## Highlights

- **The service majors (Baker Hughes, Halliburton, SLB) are the single best income-per-fit play for him right now.** Field-engineer tracks are USD-linked, pay zone + rotation bonuses, are explicitly aimed at *young* electromechanical/industrial engineers, and reward exactly what he offers: mobility, willingness to rotate, English, and a strong design/maintenance base. Baker Hughes even runs a named **"Field Engineering I — Argentina 2026" early-career program** — the cleanest on-ramp for his seniority.
- **His Tassaroli medium-voltage migration + microgrid (solar PV / BESS / gas) experience is a genuine differentiator** for electrical/energy specialist roles (YPF Electrical Energy Specialist, Pampa electrical maintenance). Few candidates his age have touched MV distribution + microgrid coordination + EDEMSA permitting.
- **Vaca Muerta salary context (May–2026 figures):** sector floor ~ARS 3.9M/month; technical maintenance ARS 5.5–9M; production engineers 6–8.5M; senior drilling engineers 8.5–11M; Tecpetrol positions can exceed 10.5M; management (PMO/Ops) 12–17M. Neuquén roles add **zone bonuses + 14x7 / 7x7 rotation uplifts** on top. (Sources below.)
- **Mining/lithium (Puna) is a real, under-fished alternative:** electromechanical + maintenance + safety are named as the *hardest-to-fill* profiles, with ~1% informality and field-supervisor pay of ARS 6–8.5M, on a steep growth curve (Morgan Stanley: ~12,000 new mining-construction jobs projected for 2026). Remote-camp rotational suits his mobility.
- **Vista Energy:** no specific open engineering vacancy surfaced in search (careers at vistaenergy.com/nuestra-gente), but it's aggressively scaling toward 100k boe/d in 2026 — worth a direct-portal watch. **CGC / Shell / TotalEnergies / NOV / Weatherford:** no specific current postings surfaced this pass; recommend direct careers-portal + LinkedIn alerts.

## Best 2–3 income plays (recommendation)

1. **Baker Hughes "Field Engineering I — Argentina 2026" (Neuquén).** Highest fit×income: USD-linked service-co pay, built for his exact seniority, rewards his mobility/rotational openness. Apply first.
2. **Halliburton Wireline & Perforating engineer (Neuquén)** and **SLB field-engineer track (apply.slb.com, filter Neuquén).** Same high-income service-co lane; Halliburton *explicitly* lists electromechanical engineers and had short-term start dates. Apply to both to widen the funnel.
3. **YPF Electrical / Energy Specialist** *(if the separate PAE/YPF lane hasn't already claimed it)* **or Pampa Energía electrical maintenance** — the roles that best monetize his rare MV-migration + microgrid experience. High income, and the strongest "his experience is uniquely relevant" story of the list. **[MAT?]** flag on the YPF AEA-design variant.

**Cross-cutting apply tips:** lead every application with (a) full relocation + offshore/rotational availability, (b) certified English + Oxford C1 scheduled Nov 2026, (c) the MV-migration/microgrid project, and (d) hands-on PLC + maintenance planning (5S, work orders on critical O&G equipment). These four are what push him up the service-company and operator shortlists.
